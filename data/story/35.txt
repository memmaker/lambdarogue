With a sharp buzzing, the android falls down on his feet. Metal hits metal,
and machine oil is already bubbling out of artificial wounds. In its struggle
with 'death', it is uttering a stream of words, fragments of sentences, that
are merely confusing:

"Defence systems are damaged...effectiveness down to 70%...starboard engines
started...evasive maneuvers... ... ...port engines malfunction...compensating
...fire tubes 2 and 3...security password: DX57AC01 ...ETA 3 minutes..."

The mechanical voice of the android gets lower and slower with every second,
but it is hard not to listen. "Do you understand what it's saying?" Suddenly
you hear the deep voice of the commander behind you. "Not really." Indeed,
to you it makes not much sense. It sounds like a description of a battle, but
why is the android reciting it now? Is the machine seeing its life rushing
by, like a dying human being in its last moments?

"Come", says the commander and touches you on your shoulder. "The weapons
officer might need your help, too. He's trying to get access to the weapon
systems of this ship, but he's stuck." You nod and follow the commander.
