Carefully you listen to the near and distant sounds, while climbing down
the stairs. Somehow you expect to hear that voice again, apparently the
voice of the Undying King, leader of the Forgotten -- sinister and almost
undead -- Realm.

You are not disappointed. "You made it to my estate" it says. "I'm waiting
for you. Find me in the mansion, face my powers and show your strength.
But beware: Eris, the lost deity, will force me to defend myself and do
harm to you. If you feel not ready for a battle against all elements, re-
turn to your own world and come back later.

You MUST overcome me, I beg you. Just redeem me of my curse. Just let me
die." The king's last words are followed by a deep groaning, and for a se-
cond you hear him summon something. It seems to you that Eris, the goddess
of hate and despair, is now in control of him.

You decide to fulfill the wish of the king. To transform the Undying King
to a dying one.
