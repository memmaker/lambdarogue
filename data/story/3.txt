Cautiously, you approach the small woman who is standing somewhat lost in the
corridor. Even more cautiously she looks at you. Obviously she's wondering if
you're a danger or merely an unexpected event in the darkness of this strange
place, with its humid, but cold air.

You try to smile, in order to reduce her fear. "Hello", the woman whispers. It
seems she has not spoken for a long time. "Who are you? What do you want?" she
asks. You explain why you have entered the depths of the sewers and tell her
about your quest.

The woman slowly nods. "I think you have come to a good place to start. You
surely have noticed all these bookshelves, and perhaps you already met my
master, the librarian." The woman twitches nervously at the sleeves of her
shirt. "I am his, well, assistant. He shares many of his secrets with me. I
know much about rare books." She looks around hectically, then comes near to
your face.
