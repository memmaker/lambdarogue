You have won the battle against the librarian. You decided not to kill him,
but he will be unconscious for a while. Now you're searching his pockets for
any hints on the "Book of Stars". After a while, you find a diary.

You flip through the densely filled pages, a crude mixture of ideas, personal
thoughts and inventory lists. Apparently the old man feels bitter about many
of the latest events in human history, and he does not agree with the
Temple's current politics. More than once you see angry exclamations, such as
"Fools!!" and "Silly infants, everybody!"

On the other hand, the librarian seems to have some feelings for his young
female assistant---which is something you did not expect at all. However,
even this he hides beneath cynicism.

"Mm..." Suddenly you hear murmuring. Is the librarian already awake again?
Faster than before you search the diary, and finally you find something. An
entry clearly speaking of the Book of Stars. You cut out the relevant pages
and put the diary back into the librarian's cape. You then retreat, watching
the librarian as he stands up, confused and shaking his head while slowly
moving out of sight. "Silly heroes", you hear him whisper...
