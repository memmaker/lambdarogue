They must not find it! If they would, everything would change. Our whole life
would become irrelevant. I have to stop them, I have to protect the book,
thereby protecting our predestined future.

The silly woman does not understand. I do not doubt her good intentions, but
she does not know even a glimpse of the truth. I therefore have not only era-
sed every hint of the book at the library. I even have, may the Gods forgive
me, burned the last Book of Stars. It was a shame, but I did it in order to
safe our lives...(though I often doubt if that has been enough...and it is
so long ago, by now...)

However, Yron was wise. He included hints on the Book of Stars in his Minor
Books, and it is even possible to re-create the essence of the Major Book
by combining the Minor Books. I am the only one with the key and because I've
got to protect us, I moved three of the Minor Books to the lost outpost, the
caves and the dark temple. The fourth, however, is not so easily obtained.
I sacrificed it. Only the blood of Ares, combined with the enchanted waters,
can bring it back to the mortals. Thus, the Book of Stars is protected for-
ever.
