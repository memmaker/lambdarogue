Tears are a mystery.

We cry because we love.
We cry because we hate.
We cry in joy, we cry in pain, and sometimes even the silence of the dungeons
seems to sound like tears, like water which is flowing down the river.

Tears are reason and consequence.
They are reason for wars and reason for peace, and they follow war and they
follow peace.

Life therefore is a circle of Tears, and unlike some old religions tell, we
mustn't leave that circle. If we did, we are not men any longer.
