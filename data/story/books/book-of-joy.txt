Mankind's best property is it's ability to feel, and it's greatest works were
made with one particular feeling: Joy. Now, as we have lost the Great War and
as we are banned from Earth, we might lose this feeling, and therefore might
also lose our power for greatness. This is why I write this book.

A priest of the Drekh'Nar once wrote:

    Truth is a river, and God is the mouth of the river.

Although the belief that there's only one god is truly misled by false inter-
pretations, this is nevertheless a wise statement.

If Joy is the river we follow to create wonders, we have to remember the words
of Dionysa, who said to Hermes:

    The Creator gave us the ability to indulge ourselves
    in uncountable pleasures---why shouldn't we use this,
    to the advantage of all creation?
