PATH OF ENCHANTERS

The Council of Enchanters and Mages (CEM) is a loose organisation of most of
the enchanters, mages and believers in our country. The council supports the
other orders -- the Order of Archers and Rangers (OAR) and the Order of Sol-
diers (OOS) -- with their tasks, but our members are proud to be independent.

Members can decide freely about their field of interest. We even have members
who are -- at the same time -- battle mage and holy warrior, which of course
makes a very powerful combination of powers.
