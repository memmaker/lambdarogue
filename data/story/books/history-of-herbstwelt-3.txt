Over the centuries, debates about accepting or rejecting mankinds fate have
become much quieter in Herbstwelt. Most people accept the fact that men
live in a somehow condensed world (not very different from the Dwarves from
the mythical era, as some historians point out). As executive branch of the
--nowadays very secluded-- Council, the Temple of Enoa is the de facto govern-
ment of Herbstwelt. The respective priestess of the Temple --currently this
is Abbess Ahna-- and her advisory board claim the right to define general
guidelines for politics, safety, and education. With the help of the Order of
Soldiers (OOS), which supports the Temple since its beginnings, the Temple is
able to enforce the law. Most people accept guidance by the Temple, as this
has created a society based on respect for life and belief in the 5 divines,
but which is still open both for political adjustments to the people's needs,
and for scientific advancements.

On the other hand, the growing power of the Temple disturbs some people. In
recent years, several small groups have gained influence. Especially the
"Rubyred Apostolate", currently led by Apostle Barfaee, tries to motivate
people in rejecting the status quo and instead gain power to overcome "man-
kind's prison". In fact, in many speaches Barfaee calls for war, to take re-
venge on the Drekh'Nar. The Temple of Enoa is watching these developments at-
tentively, but is convinced that currently the Apostolate is not a threat.
