PATH OF ARCHERS

The Order of Archers and Ranger (OAR) has been founded shortly after the Out-
skirts of Enoa were formed by our ancestors. Members of OAR care both about
the preservation of the beautiful nature of our underground country, esp. the
Outskirts, and support of members of other orders, such as the OOS with
police tasks (but the latter only when explicitly asked by OOS members).

Members can decide freely in which area to specialize. In contrast to the OOS,
but similar to CEM, we don't have a supreme leader or grandmaster; we believe
one person alone is not able to decide for a whole group.
