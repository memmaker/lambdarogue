After Ares showed himself to Men and the Drekh'Nar, a more peaceful world was
created. There were still wars, but fought quickly, with honor and regarding
rules of humanity which Ares had given to the people. At the same time, people
developed great skills and founded sciences, while the importance of the gods
decreased.

Most gods found a way to get along with that development, but Eris, deity of
dispute and chaos, was scared of being forgotten. Vixenish as always, she cre-
ated a plan to divide the people of Earth again, to enforce chaos and war and
stall the development of Earth. She wanted to be worshipped at all costs, and
she denied a possible higher destiny for men and the Drekh'Nar.

Eris seeded mistrust and misunderstanding within and among the species, and
the results of her efforts are known---men and Drekh'Nar started the greatest
war of all time, and when it was over, men were forced to go underground.

Since then, the sad times have never ended.
