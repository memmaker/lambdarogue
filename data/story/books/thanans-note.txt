Dear Patron,

when you receive this letter, the honorable task you asked me to do will al-
ready be done. In the last weeks, I was very successful with performing some
very powerful evocations--even more powerful than those I demonstrated to you
in my last visit to the Apostolate's monastery.

However, I must warn you: I believe the prior of the Enoa Temple has a vague
idea of my secret experiments. I also believe that he told the abbess. I have
to be very cautious in the next days, or they might catch me. Perhaps they
even asked someone to bring me down. At the moment, I feel very uncomfortable
here, and you might consider moving the monastery away from the Forgotten
Realm to another place soon. Forgive me, Patron. I'm pretentious in giving
you advice. For sure, the Apostolate itself knows better than me what to do.

I must stop now. I will make sure you receive this note as soon as possible.
Don't try to contact me afterwards--they will be hunting me, and I don't want
them to direct their attention towards the Apostolate. Morning Star must be
secure.

T.
