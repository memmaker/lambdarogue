Hate is understandable.
Though, hate is not the way for us to go.
At least, if we want to leave this place one day.
If we want to come back to Earth,
If we want to reach high into the skies.

Yes, the Drekh'Nar have won that war ages ago. They forced us to live in caves,
build tunnels through the darkness, where monsters are just waiting for us, to
kill us.

But undoubtedly: If we reach surface one day, we will have to face the
Drekh'Nar, and while they might still be dangerous, and while they might try to
force us back, we can't fight against them.

It was hate that brought the war---a terrible misunderstanding beget from fear
and mistrust. We must not repeat that.

So here's the way to prevent hate for us all.
