Men and the species of Drekh'Nar once lived peaceful together on Earth,
both not far away from reaching the stars with spaceships. Sadly, a mis-
understanding led to war before these dreams could be realized. After a
long and bloody battle, a peace treaty was created. It forced mankind to
go underground, while the Drekh'nar stayed on the surface of Earth.

Since then, ages have passed. Both species haven't seen each other, and for
many people, the Drekh'Nar are just legends. Mankind has lost most of its
technological knowledge, but it has developed a civilization based on re-
spect and belief. Life was peaceful again, for a long time.

However, some days ago, an ancient space ship has been found, the "Valeri".
Ahna, a priestess in one of the underground settlements, believes that the
legendary Book of Stars may contain information on this issue.

The Book of Stars was written by Yron shortly after the war, to preserve
knowledge that might otherwise get lost. Unfortunately the book disappeared
some centuries ago, but now, after long research, Ahna has found hints that
may lead to the discovery of the Book of Stars, and therefore to the
rediscovery of Earth, and Sky, and Space.
