Using the cube which you got from Alvin, you deactivate the force field
that is protecting the ladder to the "Valeri", the ancient space ship. As
soon as you climb down the ladder, dry and stale air welcomes you.

Now you're standing in a hallway. The floor is a grid of an unknown metal,
and the walls are made of something that doesn't even remotely remind you of
any materials you know.

In the distance, you recognize movements. First, you think these are the
human workers ordered to inspect the "Valeri", but as they come nearer, you
identify them as machines. Robots, which clearly make you shiver. Luckily,
they seem to ignore you, as long as you ignore them.

You also recognize additional force fields, obviously separating different
sections and rooms of the ship, and therefore working as 'doors'. However,
you better not stand inside such a 'door' when the force field gets active.

Finally, you decide to carry on. Afterall, you have to get to the commander.
