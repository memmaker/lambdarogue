...silence

.....Silence, heat, and a smell of fire and sulfur...but your skin is
cold like ice. You look around and smile. Meteor...you summoned this in-
credible power of space---and it came, directly to Earth, on your own com-
mand!

What you can see above your head is unbelievable, and it was you who created
this: a way to freedom. "Finally...the border has fallen..." you whis-
per and hold your sword tight to your body. "Now the way to vengeance is open
and I will lead us to glory!" You shout the last words, attracting your for-
mer friends and comrades.

You laugh and glance around, eagerly. You recognize the faces, but they mean
nothing to you. One of them, Ahna, steps forward. Fear and anger are on her
face.

"It is the wrong way" she says, but you do not even hear her.
