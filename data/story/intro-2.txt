Mankind has lost the most of its technological knowledge, but it has
developed a civilization based on respect on belief.

Again, life is peaceful.

However, some people are still striving for more. One of them is Ahna,
priestess in one of the underground settlements. Since her youth, she was
interested in the old stories, in dreams of Sky and Stars.

Her whole life Ahna has researched on a legendary book written shortly after
the war:

The "Book of Stars".

Some days ago, our archaeologists have found an old space ship. We believe
that the Book of Stars contains information on this issue.

Ahna wants you to help us to find this important book.
