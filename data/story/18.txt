About two hours later you sit down. The energy in your body has been re-
placed by cold reason. If you will find the Drekh'Nar anywhere, then it
will be on the surface of Earth. Thus, you have to reach it somehow. In
some old corners of your memory you remember legends once told by Ahna, the
fatuous priestess in the Temple.

Ah, she knew lots of old stories, and probably wants to be the main
character of her own story one day. That damn woman was always so egocentric
and selfish...you once admired her, but now you see her limited horizon.

However, in one of these legends a mighty power from outside the planet was
mentioned. When you heard the story that day it sounded unbelievable, but
now, with the might of 'Northdoom' in your hands, it's not that far away
from reason. Perhaps the Drekh'Nar themselves are not from your own planet,
but invaders from the outside?

Whatever. This power Ahna talked about was called 'Meteor', and it is still
hidden somewhere beneath the Temple. Logic tells you that it must be buried
very deep, probably deeper than mankind ever discovered. You decide to
search for it and then use it, to destroy the Temple's false goals and make
the way open, to the surface of Earth and future of men.
