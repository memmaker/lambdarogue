Ahna has waited for you. With a friendly smile she steps forward and greets
you.

"Ah, there you are, my friend. I am very proud that YOU are the one who
helps me with getting Yron's Book of Stars." Her eyes sparkle. It is the
same sparkle you saw when Ahna welcomed you as novice in the Temple of Enoa.
Since then you have become a close friend to her.

"Listen", she speaks. "It might be possible that the old librarian has some
information concerning our task. However, there's a problem with him." Ahna
sighs. "Long ago he decided to live as an outcast and went away from our
temple, down to the sewers. He won't talk about the Book. I even fear he
won't talk about anything at all, but be angry about your presence."

Ahna's face shows worry. "You surely want to investigate this possibility,
but please be careful while doing so. The librarian has an assistant. I do
not know him personally, but perhaps he can be helpful."

You smile at Ahna and assure her that you won't fail in your quest.
