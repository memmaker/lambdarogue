Being a thief is not easy, and you often thought of chucking this job. The
income can be great, but it also has disadvantages---like being caught by
the guards. So you went to the Temple years ago and Ahna became a close
friend. However, somehow your past does not leave you. You still have the
desire to steal, just for the bittersweet feeling. And sometimes...well,
sometimes it leads you to old relationships, old friends, and old scores.

Now you are here, facing Tony, the younger brother of Silvio Capresi, the
Godfather. You haven't seen him for years, and you really did not intend
to change this. You wanted to forget that part of your past. However, ob-
viously this plan did not work out.

"Tony." Brown strained eyes, taking a drag of his forbidden cigarette. Then
a slight nod, followed by exhaling the smoke. "Do you truly think it
was a good idea to come back?" Tony's voice is soft, friendly, just a bit
older than the last time you saw him---when you left the Capresi family.

You need the help of the family. Their contacts and knowledge about rare
goods should help you in finding the Book of Stars. Tony listens. "Okay.
I'll check with the Godfather. We'll see. Wait here."
