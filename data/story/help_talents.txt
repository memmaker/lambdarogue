TALENTS, DIVINE RAGE, AND DISTRESS

Each profession offers a "talent" as special action which consists of 4 or 5
powers. Powers are learned by gaining ranks within your character's profes-
sion. A soldier, for example, can gain the ranks Centurio, Hastatus, Prin-
ceps, Pilus, and finally Primus Pilus. Each rank of a profession's career
has one associated power, either active or passive. The talent you can use
is the cumulated effect of all active powers. Passive powers improve status
values of your character. The 1st power of a talent is available for free
right from the start. Press the talent key (default: [x]) to use the talent.

Each religion has one associated "divine rage". Like the profession talent,
this is a special action you can use when needed, but divine rage is not di-
vided into powers. You can increase the effect and reduce the costs of di-
vine rage by training your Humility skill. To invoke divine rage, press the
divine rage key (default: [X]).

Both powers and divine rage cost "distress" (DST). You gain distress by ta-
king damage. When you use your talent or invoke divine rage, DST is reduced
by the DST cost of the talent's powers or the divine rage.
