For a long time, mankind and Drekh'Nar lived peacefully together on Earth.
Men lived on a continent called Herbstwelt. Many green fields and beautiful
valleys with lots of resources allowed for a comfortable life.

Both religion and science prospered. Finally, mankind and Drekh'Nar were
even about to conquer space.

Sadly, a misunderstanding led to war between the species, before the dreams
of space could be realized. The war was long, and the battles were bloody.

Finally, a peace treaty was signed. The treaty forced mankind to go under-
ground, while the Drekh'Nar could stay on the surface of Earth.

Since then, ages have passed. Both species haven't seen each other and for
many people, the Drekh'Nar are just legends.
