The commander, Ahna, and you are standing in the former caves, outside of the
"Valeri". While you're looking towards the now visible sky, the commander
explains: "The ship crashed and made the Drekh'Nar believe in the end of the
'Valeri'.

Well, this was long ago. Today, the 'Valeri' will rise again." You nod. Ahna
says: "We should go", and so you enter the ship.

All bulkheads get closed, the engines start, everything rumbles. Only a minute
later you're up in the air. Higher and higher the "Valeri" flys, and soon it
leaves Earth and Sky behind and enters space.

For the first time, you can see not only the surface, and the sky, but even
the whole planet from above. You shiver.
