As you return to Barfaee, holding the powerful sword Northdoom in your hands,
you feel unsure if this was the best decision. It's not that you could have
used the sword for yourself (though this would probably had given you a tre-
mendous amount of power), but why you haven't destroyed the sword in lava, or
not even went out to find it, but simply told Ahna or Prior Marcus about it...
Probably it's the old problem: to be unable to reject other people's requests.
Now there's no way around it. Barfaee expects you to give Northdoom to him,
and he's already staring suspiciously at you. "Come, Novice. Come and give me
that sword" he speaks. On the surface, his voice is calm and friendly, but
his eyes show ecstatic wildness, jealousy, greed, and distrust. You breathe
out, then step forward, kneel, and lay down the sword in front of the apostle.
You stand up, step back and look at him. Barfaee smiles broadly. Faster than
you ever saw him moving before he graps the sword. Suddenly, the air gets
cold. Soon, you won't be sweating anymore. You wait. For several minutes,
Barfaee does not seem to recognize you, as he only stares at the sword, tou-
ches it, smiles and breathes. Finally he looks at you again. "Why are you do-
ing this?" he asks. "I KNOW that it was not your wish to let me keep the
sword! In fact, by now I know that your so-called devotion to the One is not
even remotely true!!" He raises his arms and chants a conjuration song. "The
One has sent a judge", he says after some seconds. "Astaroth will weigh your
sins and punish you for your lies!" Barfaee starts to laugh demonically.
