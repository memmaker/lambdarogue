Attracted by the noise, Prior Marcus arrives. "What has happened?" he asks.
"Don't worry any longer about Thanan. He's dead." Your voice sounds bitter,
and you feel ashamed of killing one of your own Temple brothers. Marcus nods.
"So Thanan is stopped. That's...good. Thanan had so many abilities...but I
think he decided long ago that he won't follow the way of the Temple forever."
Prior Marcus sighs, then he says:

"I ask myself why he went here to perfom his experiments." Carefully Marcus
examines the crypt. "There's nothing special about it, except..." He frowns,
then gets excited: "Wait...in this hollow in the wall...something metallic."
Prior Marcus grabs into the hole and some seconds later he returns with a
small plate. "What's that?" you ask, but Prior Marcus shrugs. "I really don't
know, but there's an engraving, like a map, and some words: 'A gift by Eris,
only for the brave'. Eris...the lost deity. She doesn't live in the heavens
like the other gods, but instead went to the core of Earth, waiting, lurking.
According to this map, the way to her is deep down, in the Dark Temple." The
prior shivers. "I wonder who gave Thanan this plate." For some seconds, he
seems clueless, but then his face brightens. "Come, to my library! Some days
ago, we have found a note in Thanan's room. It might be interesting!"
