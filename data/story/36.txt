You have reached the second deck of the "Valeri", in the heart of the ship.
While the first deck contained several small rooms, the second deck consists
of the command center as well as the port and starboard engine rooms. Most
probably, the commander is inside the command center.

If the Book of Stars is correct, men stopped to build space ships shortly af-
ter the beginning of the war against the Drekh'Nar, and soon all existing
ships got destroyed. Somehow, men must have been able to hide the "Valeri"
before it was overtaken by the same fate.

As you slowly continue to wander through the hallways, you wonder how the
"Valeri" was transported so deep beneath the surface of Earth. You actually
don't know where exactly you are; 'somewhere' near the Forgotten Realm, which
in turn is 'somewhere' below the Outskirts of Enoa. You know that the Out-
skirts are partly artificial--perhaps there existed a connection to the sur-
face of Earth when men went underground, and they hid the ship in the caves
before the connection was closed?

Interesting ideas, but you may never really get to know the truth about it.
So you continue with your task.
