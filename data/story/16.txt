While stepping down the stairs you already feel that something's in disor-
der here, even more than usual. Then you reach the next level of this
crude maze and see the body of a young woman laying on the ground. She
looks well-trained, but is motionless now. You run in her direction and
kneel down, in order to check her pulse.

Nothing. The low temperature of her skin confirms this sign. The young wo-
man is dead, for several hours now. You have seen so much death in the caves
and dungeons below the Temple of Enoa that another dead body could be noth-
ing special anymore. This, however, is different---a young, beautiful wo-
man who probably did no harm to anybody. You sigh sadly.

A quick check of her pockets tells you her name. "Lassa Kana", the identity
card says. "Resident of the Lost Outpost." You have been there and talked
to her mother, who had been worried about her daughter. Now you both know
the answer.

Then you remember the words of Lassa's mentor. Prof. Zimmerman told you
about a crystal Lassa was searching for. Again you examine her pockets, and
verily, you find something. You should take it with you and show it to the
professor.
