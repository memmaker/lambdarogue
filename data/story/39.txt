While you approach Barfaee, the leader of the Rubyred Apostolate, you feel
dozens of eyes watching you. Apparently the novices, zealots, and priests are
not used to someone speaking directly with their apostle. Are they jealous?
Or simply curious? On your side is Patron Merapis. "You should kneel", he
whispers, just before you stop in front of Barfaees throne. 'Kneel?' you
think. 'This is not something we are used to in the Temple of Enoa.' Every-
thing inside you wants to resist this order, but the Patron is already sin-
king down. To avoid raising suspicion, you do the same. From the corner of
the eye you see Barfaee nodding, satisfied. "Stand up", the apostle speaks,
and his voice resounds with the walls. 'Impressive', you think, only partly
ironically, as you notice a little worried. "So you are the one everybody is
talking about. I don't know you yet, but the One does. And she sent me a vi-
sion" --now he's speaking not only to you, but to the crowd-- "about your role
in the glorious path that lies ahead of us. The One promised starlight to us!
She gave us a true understanding of Yron's Book of Stars, not the false one of
the Temple of Enoa's traitors!" His voice is shouting now dramatically. "The
end of our oppression is near! We'll overcome the old gods who created the
Drekh'Nar brood, we will overcome the false leadership of the Council and the
Temple!---Soon, we will see the Morning Star again!" The crowd is shouting
ecstatically now, but Barfaee stops them. With a quiet voice again, he adds:
"And the Drekh'Nar...will regret forever what they have done to our race."
