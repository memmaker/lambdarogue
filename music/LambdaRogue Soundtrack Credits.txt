LambdaRogue Soundtrack (1.4)
----------------------------

The soundtrack of LambdaRogue consists totally of songs licensed under
Creative Commons, allowing me to redistribute them freely in noncommercial
projects.

Note that due to some limitations of the Windows version of mpg123, I had
to strip all ID3-tags from the MP3 files. Therefore here are the full
credits (including links to the websites where you can download the songs).
